Theoretically, if the copy number of each allele is the same, the CPM value from allele 1 and allele 2 should be equal. If that is not the case and one allele showed more coverage than the other allele, it indicates that a chromosome copy-number difference occurred. The fold change in the CPM value on two alleles (allele 2 CPM/allele 1 CPM < 0.8 or allele 2 CPM/allele 1 CPM > 1.2) was used to evaluate the outliers of chromosome coverage.
