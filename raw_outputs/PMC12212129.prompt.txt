Discussion In this study, we have shown that hTSCs can be derived from term CTBs under atmospheric oxygen conditions in TUA medium; TUA medium is TSCM used for derivation of hTSCs from first-trimester placentas and blastocyst-stage embryos described by Okae et al. Although term CTBs could be cultured for a few passages in TSCM under atmospheric oxygen conditions, they could not be maintained in long-term culture.
