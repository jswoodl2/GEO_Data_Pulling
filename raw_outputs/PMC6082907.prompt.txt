However, because the proportions of the different types of known placental cells are so different in the placenta, an unbiased selection strategy would probably miss the potentially important but rare populations. We chose not to use fluorescence-activated cell sorting (FACS) technology because the primary cells, especially placenta-derived trophoblast cells, are very vulnerable to damage and the high speeds experienced by cells during FACS can be detrimental (data not shown).
