This study demonstrates the value of an in vitro pluripotent stem cell model designed to examine early TB development in pregnancies that had been complicated by EOPE. The basis of this model is the generation of iPSCs from UC of infants born to mothers who experienced EOPE during the course of their pregnancies. These cell lines may be a useful resource for others wishing to study this enigmatic disease.
