At the molecular level, Klf5 participates in TSC core regulation by occupying proximal promoter regions and maintaining an open chromatin structure of its target TSC pluripotency genes. In addition, histone modification of H3K27ac mediated by Klf5 was required for the maintenance of TSCs and proper mouse peri-implantation development. Together, our findings provide new evidence that Klf5 is essential for both the establishment and maintenance of TSC pluripotency, extending the understanding of the TSC core regulatory network.
