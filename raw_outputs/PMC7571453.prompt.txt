Objective Investigate the impact of fetal sex on maternal-fetal crosstalk. Design Receptors/ligands at the maternal-fetal surface were identified from sexually dimorphic genes between fetal sexes in the first trimester placenta and defined in each cell type using single-cell RNA-Sequencing (scRNA-Seq).
