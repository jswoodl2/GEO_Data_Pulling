Trisomy13, Trisomy 18 and Trisomy 21 are the most common, clinically relevant fetal aneusomies. This study used a transcriptomics approach to identify the molecular signatures at the maternal-fetal interface in each aneuploidy. Methods We profiled placental gene expression (13–22 wks) in T13 (n = 4), T18 (n = 4), and T21 (n = 8), and in euploid pregnancies (n = 4). Results We found differentially expressed (DE) transcripts (≥ 2-fold) in T21 (n = 160), T18 (n = 80), and T13 (n=125). A smaller number of the DE transcripts were encoded on the trisomic chromosome, suggesting gene dosage. In T21, <10% of the genes were transcribed from the Down syndrome critical region (21q21-22), which contributes to the clinical phenotype. In T13, 15% of the upregulated genes were on the affected chromosome (13q11-14) and in T18 the percentage increased to 24% (18q11-22 region).
