Cell lysis, sonication, immunoprecipitation, and cleanup steps were all performed at 4 °C. Finally, chromatin was eluted from beads using elution buffer (1% SDS, 0.1M NaHCO3) and protein-DNA crosslinks were reversed with the addition of 5M NaCl, and DNA was purified using Qiaquick column cleanup (Qiagen). 50-500 ng immunoprecipitated DNA was prepared for sequencing using the Illumina genomic DNA preparation kit.
