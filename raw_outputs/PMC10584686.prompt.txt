In brief, confocal microscopy was used to localize the chosen SEM (the previously gathered overview helped guide the stage to the right direction). The live view was used to ensure that the correct SEM was selected. Using the binocular view and a mouth pipette (with a previously pulled glass capillary), the structure was taken from the plate while trying to avoid disturbing other structures as little as possible and without generating bubbles. The picked SEM was placed in a new drop of PBS on a Petri dish for further analysis.
