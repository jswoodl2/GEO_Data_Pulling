(G) Quantification of KI67 immunostaining in HV + − See also Figure S5 Figure 6 Model Comparing Standard Serum/LIF ESC Culture to 2i ESC Culture The model relates serum/LIF and 2i ESC cultures to embryonic development. 2i/LIF cultures contain totipotent single cells that are comparable to early preimplantation stages when embryonic cells are still totipotent. As Fgf is needed for later lineage segregation, inhibiting Fgf signaling in 2i seems to block embryos and ESCs at a point prior to this commitment event. Serum/LIF ESC cultures are reminiscent of later embryonic stages when cells are pluripotent and contain more restricted cell types. Although partially differentiated mesoderm and ectoderm appear to be lost in 2i/LIF, endoderm is maintained. Based on our data, totipotent ESCs are able to dynamically interconvert to a pluripotent epiblast precursor state and may also exist in equilibrium with a similar primitive endoderm precursor, although we have not been able to distinguish this population in this study. LIF supports the expansion of the totipotent ESC state and primitive endoderm precursors in serum.
