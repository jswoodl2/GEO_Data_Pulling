Summary Mammalian blastocyst formation involves the specification of the trophectoderm followed by the differentiation of the inner cell mass into embryonic epiblast and extra-embryonic primitive endoderm (PrE). During this time, the embryo maintains a window of plasticity and can redirect its cellular fate when challenged experimentally. In this context, we found that the PrE alone was sufficient to regenerate a complete blastocyst and continue post-implantation development. In mouse, the 2-cell stage blastomere is able to generate a complete organism from a single cell, 1 , 2 3 , 4 , 5 , 6 , 7 Lineage specification begins after morula compaction with the acquisition of polarity in the outer cells that form the extra-embryonic trophectoderm (TE) surrounding the inner cell mass (ICM).
